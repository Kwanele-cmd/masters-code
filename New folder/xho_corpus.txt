Ndihlala eKhayelitsha kunye nosapho lwam, apho sichitha ixesha elininzi sithetha ngezinto ezibalulekileyo ebomini bethu.
Umama upheka isidlo sangokuhlwa ekhitshini ngelixa abantwana besenza umsebenzi wesikolo kwigumbi lokuhlala.
Utata usebenza edolophini yonke imihla ukuze akwazi ukuxhasa usapho lwethu nokuhlawula iindleko zendlu.
Nangona imvula ina kakhulu namhlanje, abantu basaya emsebenzini kuba kufuneka bagqibe imisebenzi yabo.
Abafundi abafunda isiXhosa esikolweni bakhuthazwa ukuba babhale amabali aza kupapashwa kwiphephandaba lesikolo.
Utitshala uchaza isifundo esitsha ngendlela enomdla ukuze abafundi baqonde kakuhle oko kufundiswayo.
Ndifunda nzima ebusuku kuba ndifuna ukuphumelela iimviwo zokugqibela zonyaka.
Iyunivesithi yaseMpuma Koloni inika abafundi amathuba amaninzi ophando nolwazi olunzulu.
Abahlali bale ndawo badibene kwiholo yoluntu ukuze baxoxe ngeengxaki zamanzi nombane ezichaphazela wonke umntu.
Urhulumente ubhengeze umthetho omtsha oza kunceda amashishini amancinci akhule aze aqeshe abantu abaninzi.
Iqela lebhola lasekuhlaleni liphumelele umdlalo obalulekileyo emva kokuba liqeqeshe nzima iinyanga ezininzi.
Abantu abaninzi bafudukela esixekweni bekhangela amathuba emisebenzi angcono kunye nobomi obungcono.
Ugqirha undinike amayeza athile kwaye wandicebisa ukuba ndiphumle iintsuku ezimbalwa ukuze ndiphile ngokupheleleyo.
Isibhedlele esitsha esakhiwe kufutshane nedolophu sinceda abantu abaninzi abafuna unyango olukhawulezayo.
