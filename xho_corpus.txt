Ndihlala eKhayelitsha kunye nosapho lwam, apho sichitha ixesha elininzi sithetha ngezinto ezibalulekileyo ebomini bethu.
Umama upheka isidlo sangokuhlwa ekhitshini ngelixa abantwana besenza umsebenzi wesikolo kwigumbi lokuhlala.
Utata usebenza edolophini yonke imihla ukuze akwazi ukuxhasa usapho lwethu nokuhlawula iindleko zendlu.
Nangona imvula ina kakhulu namhlanje, abantu basaya emsebenzini kuba kufuneka bagqibe imisebenzi yabo.
Abafundi abafunda isiXhosa esikolweni bakhuthazwa ukuba babhale amabali aza kupapashwa kwiphephandaba lesikolo.
Utitshala uchaza isifundo esitsha ngendlela enomdla ukuze abafundi baqonde kakuhle oko kufundiswayo.
Ndifunda nzima ebusuku kuba ndifuna ukuphumelela iimviwo zokugqibela zonyaka.
Iyunivesithi yaseMpuma Koloni inika abafundi amathuba amaninzi ophando nolwazi olunzulu.
Abahlali bale ndawo badibene kwiholo yoluntu ukuze baxoxe ngeengxaki zamanzi nombane ezichaphazela wonke umntu.
Urhulumente ubhengeze umthetho omtsha oza kunceda amashishini amancinci akhule aze aqeshe abantu abaninzi.
Iqela lebhola lasekuhlaleni liphumelele umdlalo obalulekileyo emva kokuba liqeqeshe nzima iinyanga ezininzi.
Abantu abaninzi bafudukela esixekweni bekhangela amathuba emisebenzi angcono kunye nobomi obungcono.
Ugqirha undinike amayeza athile kwaye wandicebisa ukuba ndiphumle iintsuku ezimbalwa ukuze ndiphile ngokupheleleyo.
Isibhedlele esitsha esakhiwe kufutshane nedolophu sinceda abantu abaninzi abafuna unyango olukhawulezayo.
Ukutya okunempilo kunye nokuzilolonga rhoqo kunceda abantu baphile ubomi obude nobusempilweni.
IsiXhosa lulwimi olutyebileyo olunembali ende, kwaye lusetyenziswa kwimfundo, kumabonakude, nakwiincwadi ezininzi.
Amasiko ethu agcinwa ngabantu abadala abasoloko befundisa ulutsha ngokubaluleka kwenkcubeko yethu.
Umnyhadala wamasiko obubanjelwe kule mpelaveki utsale abantu abavela kwiindawo ezahlukeneyo zelizwe.
Nangona uqoqosho lukhula kancinci, kukho ithemba lokuba amathuba emisebenzi aza kwanda kwiminyaka ezayo.
Abafundi abaninzi abasebenza nzima emini nasebusuku bayaphumelela baze baqhubeke nemfundo ephakamileyo.
Umasipala uqalise inkqubo entsha yokucoca isixeko ukuze kugcinwe indalo icocekile kwaye ikhuselekile.
Abantu baxoxa ngoqoqosho lweli lizwe kuba bafuna ukuqonda indlela iinguqu ezichaphazela ngayo ubomi babo.
Umntwana ofumene isitofu sokugonya ukhuselekile kwizifo ezininzi ezinokubakho ebuntwaneni.
Abongikazi abasebenza esibhedlele banyamekela izigulana ngobubele nangomonde omkhulu.
Nangona kwakukho iingxaki ezininzi ekuqaleni, inkampani ekugqibeleni iphumelele ngenxa yomsebenzi onzima wabasebenzi bayo.
Ulwimi ludibanisa abantu abohlukeneyo kuba luvumela unxibelelwano olucacileyo nolunentsingiselo phakathi kwabo.
Amabali esintu adluliselwa kwizizukulwana ngezizukulwana ukuze kugcinwe imbali kunye nezifundo zobomi.
Abantu abadala bathi ulutsha kufuneka luhloniphe amasiko kunye nemithetho yoluntu ukuze kuhlale uxolo.
Imfundo esemgangathweni ibalulekile kuba ivula amathuba amatsha kwaye inceda abantu bafezekise amaphupha abo.
Xa abantu besebenzisana kwaye bexhasana, uluntu lukhula kwaye luphumelele ngendlela ezinzileyo.
